Ask us something else ↗
I don’t know which website I need. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us how you take orders today and whether you want customers to pay online. We’ll discuss the two options and recommend a scope that suits your business.

Does the catalog website take payments?

No. The catalog displays your products and directs customers to WhatsApp to order. If you want an integrated payment gateway and website order management, choose the ecommerce service.

Can I manage my own inventory?

Yes. Both services include inventory management through an admin panel. The ecommerce service also includes order management. We walk you through the admin as part of handover.

What if I don’t have GST yet?

Tell us about your current setup. Our catalog service is designed for sellers who want to display products and handle orders on WhatsApp without a website payment gateway. We’ll discuss your needs before agreeing on the project.

How much does a website cost, and how long does it take?

That depends on the product catalog, design, and agreed functionality. After an initial conversation, we’ll define the scope, price, and delivery milestones with you before work begins.

What do I need to get started?

Your business name, Instagram page if you have one, and a general idea of your products are enough to start a conversation. We’ll work through the product information, images, and other requirements together.
