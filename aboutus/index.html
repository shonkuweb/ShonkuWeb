Who We Are

ShonkuWeb is a specialized AI systems engineering studio. We build production-grade Customer Support AI Agents, custom multi-agent automations, and resilient web/mobile interfaces for ambitious founders and growing companies.

We don't sell vaporware or build surface-level wrappers. Every agent we deploy is grounded in proprietary vector knowledge, guarded by deterministic security layers, and tied directly into mission-critical APIs.

Inspired by Professor Shonku

Our name is a deliberate homage to Professor Trilokeshwar Shonku, the legendary scientist and polymath created by Satyajit Ray. In the stories, Professor Shonku invented futuristic marvels from his quiet laboratory in Giridih—from the autonomous robot Bidhushekhar to anti-gravity formulas and universal translators.

His spirit of fearless experimentation, scientific integrity, and audacious problem-solving guides every line of code we write and every agent architecture we deploy today.
